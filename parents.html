For parents and teachers

Simple habits that make tracing time calm and fun.

Getting ready

  • Sit up tall with feet on the floor.
  • Hold the paper still with the other hand.
  • Hold the pencil with thumb, pointer and middle finger. A short, chunky pencil suits small fingers.

During the activity

  • Keep sessions short. Ten to fifteen minutes is plenty for young children.
  • Praise effort and patience. Neat is better than fast.
  • Do one or two pages, then stop while it is still fun.
  • Finish with colouring or doodling.

Every child is different

Children develop hand skills at different speeds. LUMELO books are learning activities, not a test. If you have concerns about your child's hand skills or writing, talk to your paediatrician or an occupational therapist.
