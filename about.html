About LUMELO

Learn. Play. Grow.

LUMELO makes playful learning for children aged 2 to 6. We start with printable and paperback workbooks that build early skills such as pencil control, letters and numbers. We are also building a preschool learning app.

Our books use short pages, clear start and stop steps and a cast of friendly animals, so that children can finish a page and feel proud.

How we make our books

Text and artwork in LUMELO books are created with the help of AI tools and are checked by the LUMELO team before publication. The LUMELO logo is AI-generated.
